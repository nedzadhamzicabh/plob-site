PLOB — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

PLOB ("the game," "we," "us") is developed by Yellow Frog Development. This policy explains what information PLOB collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. It applies to PLOB on Android and iOS.

Information we do not collect

PLOB does not require account creation, does not include chat or user-generated content, and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or photos. Your game progress (best scores, mission stars, unlocked levels, achievements, statistics, chosen looks and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Consent and your choices

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: when you first open PLOB, a consent form lets you choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings ("Privacy Options").
  • iOS: PLOB asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," your IDFA is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, at any time in your device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children's privacy

PLOB is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is served through Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play's and Apple's policies for the app's declared audience.

Data retention and deletion

All game progress is stored only on your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling PLOB. This does not affect a "Remove Ads" purchase, which is linked to your Google or Apple account and can be restored after reinstalling. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the game changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
